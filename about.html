XD-MARKET brings independent Sierra Leonean sellers and curious shoppers together in one welcoming place.

Why we’re here

Good work deserves a wider market.

Sierra Leone is full of people making, growing, designing, and finding useful things. XD-MARKET is a digital marketplace built to help those independent sellers reach buyers nearby and farther away.

From Freetown to the provinces, the idea is simple: make it easier to discover local shops, understand who you are buying from, and support small businesses with every order.

Local by nature. Connected by possibility.
How we work

A marketplace built around people.

Buyers can browse by category, compare listings, and choose from available checkout methods. Sellers can apply to join, list their products after approval, manage orders, and track their earnings transparently.
